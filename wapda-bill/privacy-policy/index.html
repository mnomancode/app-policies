1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Muhammad Noman ("I", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in the WAPDA Bill mobile app (package name pk.nomanapps.wapdabill) for Android and iOS (the "App"). The App lets you save your electricity connections, check bills from Pakistani electricity distribution companies (DISCOs), and get optional reminders about your bills.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

In short:
  • There is no sign-up, no account and no server of our own. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your saved connections, bills and alert settings are stored only on your device.
  • Bill lookups go directly from your device to the official PITC bill portal.
  • Bill alerts are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you turn them on, and the App asks for notification permission only then.
  • Bill scanning with the camera happens on your device; images are not uploaded by us.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

2. Disclaimer and data source

The App is an independent app and is not a government app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to WAPDA, PITC (Power Information Technology Company), the Government of Pakistan, any electricity distribution company (such as LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, IESCO, GEPCO, PESCO, HESCO or QESCO), or any other government organization.

We do not own or control that information and cannot guarantee that it is accurate or up to date. Always confirm important details with your electricity company. The App also shows these links, and tapping one opens the page in your browser, where that website's own policies apply.

3. Information stored on your device

When you add a connection, the App saves the following in a private database on your device:

  • The reference number and electricity company you enter;
  • Any name and category (for example Home, Shop or Solar) you give the connection;
  • Bill details fetched from the bill portal (for example the consumer name and address printed on the bill, billing month, units, amount due, due date and payment status), and whether you marked a bill as paid;
  • Your alert settings for the connection: whether a due-date reminder is on and how many days before the due date, and whether new-bill alerts are on.

This data never leaves your device except for the bill lookups described in Sections 4 and 5 and when you choose to share a bill (Section 7). We have no access to it and do not store it on any server.

4. Checking bills

When you add or refresh a connection, the App sends your reference number from your device directly to bill.pitc.com.pk over a secure (HTTPS) connection to retrieve your bill, and loads company logos from the same website. As with any website, that portal receives your IP address and basic request information. Its handling of that information is governed by its own policies, not this one.

5. Bill alerts and notifications

The App offers two optional alerts for each saved connection. Both are off by default:

  • Due-date reminder – a notification a few days (1, 2, 3, 5 or 7, as you choose) before an unpaid bill is due. The reminder is scheduled on your device using the due date of the bill already saved there. It is cancelled when the bill is paid, when you turn the reminder off, or when you delete the connection.
  • New-bill alert (Android only) – a notification when next month's bill becomes available. To find it, the App runs a background check using Android's WorkManager, only while at least one connection has this alert on and your device has an internet connection. For those connections, it looks up the bill on bill.pitc.com.pk in the same way as Section 4, at most about once a day and only around the time the next bill is expected.

These are local notifications: they are created and shown by the App on your device. We do not use a push-notification service or any server of our own, and no notification content or alert setting is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions used for alerts:

Permission Why the App uses it
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+, notification permission on iOS) To show due-date reminders and new-bill alerts. The App asks for it only when you first turn an alert on; older Android versions allow notifications by default.
Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, Android) To restore scheduled reminders after your device restarts.

You can turn alerts off at any time from the App (the bell on each connection), or block the App's notifications in your device settings. If notifications are blocked, the App will not show alerts.

6. Camera and photos

The App can scan the reference number from a paper bill so you don't have to type it:

  • Camera – used only while you are on the scan screen to read the reference number.
  • Photos – used only when you choose a picture of a bill from your gallery. Only the image you pick is accessed.

Text recognition is performed on your device using Google ML Kit. The App does not upload, save or share these images. Google ML Kit may send limited, non-identifying diagnostic and usage information to Google to operate and improve the service (see ML Kit Terms).

These permissions are optional. You can deny them and type the reference number instead, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

7. Sharing and printing bills

If you use the share or print option, the App creates an image or document of your bill on your device and hands it to the app or printer you select. What happens next is controlled by that app or service.

8. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads (banner ads, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch) provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language);
  • Ad interactions (for example ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash logs related to ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App uses Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Your ad choices

  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • iOS: the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your IDFA to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • You can also manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

9. Information we do not collect

  • No account, login, name, email address or phone number;
  • No precise location (the App does not request location permission), including in the background;
  • No contacts, messages, call logs or files other than a photo you explicitly pick;
  • No notification content or alert settings: alerts stay on your device;
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, and we do not sell your information.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete a connection or bill from within the App at any time. Deleting a connection also cancels its reminders and alerts;
  • Turn off a connection's alerts at any time from the bell on that connection;
  • Remove all App data, including scheduled reminders, by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

We keep no copy on any server, so there is nothing further for us to delete. For data collected by Google through ads, please use the Google controls linked in Section 8.

11. Security

App data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and bill requests are sent over HTTPS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your device with a screen lock.

12. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience managing household electricity bills and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us.
